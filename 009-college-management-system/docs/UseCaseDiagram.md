# College Management System – Use Case Diagram

## Scope

The proposed system is a simple **College Management System** focused on the interaction between **students, lecturers, and courses**. 

The system has two actors:

- **Student**
- **Lecturer**

A student can:

- View available courses
- View course details
- Enrol in courses
- View enrolled courses

A lecturer can:

- View assigned courses
- View students enrolled in their courses
- Manage basic course information

The system is based on three core domain concepts:

- **Student**
- **Lecturer**
- **Course**

A student can enrol in courses, while a lecturer is responsible for teaching and managing the information of assigned courses.

### Student

- **View Courses** – Allows students to see the courses available in the system.
- **View Course Details** – Allows students to view information about a selected course.
- **Enroll in Course** – Allows students to enrol in a course.
- **View Enrolled Courses** – Allows students to see the courses in which they are currently enrolled.

### Lecturer

- **View Assigned Courses** – Allows lecturers to see the courses they are responsible for teaching.
- **View Enrolled Students** – Allows lecturers to see students enrolled in their assigned courses.
- **Manage Course Information** – Allows lecturers to maintain basic information about their assigned courses.
